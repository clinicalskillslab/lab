    Stage 6 · Diagnosis

    What is the Most Likely Diagnosis?

    Drawing on everything you have found — motor, cranial nerve, and speech examination. Select the single best answer.

    ✅ Left MCA Territory Cortical Infarct

    Right-sided deficits → lesion is in the left hemisphere (contralateral, above the pyramidal decussation).

    Arm and face more affected than leg → MCA territory (lateral cortex). ACA territory supplies the medial cortex and produces leg-predominant weakness.

    Expressive aphasia — non-fluent, word-finding difficulty, intact comprehension — is a signature of the dominant (left) cortex. This excludes a capsular lacunar infarct, which is a white matter relay with no cortical features.

    Right MCA is excluded by the side of deficits. ACA territory is excluded by the arm/face predominance.
